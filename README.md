## 🧪 About the Project

This project tackles **Problem Statement 2** of the KINEXUS'25 Inter IIT NIT Hackathon, in collaboration with **Godrej Industries**, to develop a **cost-effective, environmentally friendly alternative** to traditional Nickel catalysts used in the industrial hydrogenation of vegetable oils.

We propose replacing commercial Nickel catalysts (22 wt.% Ni) with a **Supported Copper Catalyst (8 wt.% Cu/SiO₂)** prepared via the **Chemisorption–Hydrolysis (CH) method**. This catalyst achieves:

- 🔬 **88% oleic acid (C18:1)** retention through precise partial hydrogenation
- ❄️ **Pour point as low as −15 °C** — ideal for biodegradable lubricants
- ♻️ **Chromium-free, non-pyrophoric** system — safer and greener
- 💰 **Lower cost** — copper is cheaper and only 8 wt.% loading is needed (vs. 22 wt.% Ni)
- ⚡ **100× higher activity** than conventional impregnation methods

## 🔑 Key Innovation

### Chemisorption–Hydrolysis (CH) Method
The CH preparation method creates **3.5 nm copper nanoparticles** with abundant "step and edge" surface sites that are **up to 100× more active** for hydrogen dissociation compared to catalysts prepared by conventional Incipient Wetness (IW) impregnation.

### Ex Situ Pre-Reduction
An **ex situ pre-reduction at 270 °C** under hydrogen — performed before contact with oil — removes all synthesis water from catalyst pores, ensuring maximum activity and long-term stability.

## 📊 Performance at a Glance

|---|---|---|
| Metal Loading | 22 wt.% | 8 wt.% |
| C18:1 (Oleic) Content | ~78% | Up to 88% |
| C18:0 (Stearic) Formation | 11% | ~2% |
| *trans*-Content | ~30% | ~15–20% |
| Pour Point | +7 °C | −12 to −15 °C |
| Safety | Pyrophoric | Non-pyrophoric, Cr-free |

## 🏭 Process Overview

1. **Feedstock Preparation** — Crude vegetable oil undergoes degumming and bleaching (140–180 °C)
2. **Hydrogenation** — Slurry-phase batch reactor at 180 °C, 6–20 bar H₂, with pre-reduced 8 wt.% Cu/SiO₂ catalyst
3. **Post-Processing** — Filtration, deodorization, and cooling to produce the final product

## 🌿 Applications

- **Biodegradable lubricants** — Maintains fluidity down to −15 °C with excellent oxidation stability
- **Biodiesel formulations** — Improved cold-flow properties and viscosity
- **Eco-friendly industrial oils** — Replacing mineral-oil-based lubricants that pollute water sources
